ok   ICACHE_REPORTED: the icache flush is measured, reported and honoured
ok   ARENA_SIZING: the arena holds both payloads, in separate buffers

ok   UI_SURFACE_FROZEN: the visible interface is byte-identical to pass 01

ok   PROTECT_OVERFLOW: the length rounding cannot overflow silently
ok   TRUNCATION_MARKED: truncated summaries are marked as truncated
